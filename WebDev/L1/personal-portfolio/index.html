Research Publication

AI-Based Marketplace Price Matching for Produce

Abhishek Patel, Sagar Chaudhary, Aditya Raj
Published in International Journal of Research Publication and Reviews (IJRPR), Vol 7, Issue 5, May 2026, pp. 10078-10086 | ISSN 2582-7421

Produce Match is an end-to-end AI system for real-time produce price matching. It combines LightGBM, LSTM and Transformer models with a multi-agent reinforcement learning engine, IoT freshness sensing and a blockchain audit layer. It achieved MAPE of 3.7%, 4.1% and 5.2% on three real-world datasets, with inference under 100 ms on edge hardware.
